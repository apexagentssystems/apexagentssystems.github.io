When this calculator is wrong

If your consultants are not capacity-constrained — if they have spare hours and the bottleneck is vacancies coming in, not time to work them — then freeing up admin time gains you very little. Fix the front of the funnel first. Automation will not sell your service for you.

It is also wrong if your fill rate is low because of candidate quality or client behaviour rather than speed. Sifting faster does not help if the applications are not there. In that case the honest advice is to spend the money on sourcing, not on us.

Around one in five agencies we run these numbers with should not buy anything from us, and we say so. A calculator that only ever returns a reason to buy is an advert, not a diagnostic.
